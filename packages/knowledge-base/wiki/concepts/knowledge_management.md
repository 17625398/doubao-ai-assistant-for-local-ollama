# Concept: Knowledge Management

Knowledge Management (KM) refers to the systematic process of creating, capturing, organizing, and sharing information to enhance learning and effectiveness. In a personal or organizational context, it involves identifying valuable insights, structuring them for reliable retrieval, and applying them to future tasks. This concept forms the backbone of any robust information ecosystem, ensuring that raw data evolves into structured, actionable wisdom.

The [[sources/test]] document demonstrates KM at its foundational stage. By ingesting a straightforward text file and automatically producing a structured [[summaries/test]], the system validates the core ingestion and indexing pipeline. This automated workflow highlights how digital tooling can streamline the KM lifecycle, from initial document capture to thematic synthesis. Effective KM relies on consistent metadata, hierarchical linking, and iterative refinement, principles that guide the continuous growth of this personal knowledge base [[index.md]].
